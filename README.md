## 🏛️ Brand Heritage & Name Reference

### 1. Etymology & Origins
The name **VASTRIÉ** (pronounced **"vas-tree-ay"**) is a synthesis of ancient textile mastery and French haute couture nomenclature:

* **VASTRA (वस्त्र):** Derived from classical Sanskrit (*Bharat*), meaning *garment*, *fabric*, and the foundational art of weaving, natural dyeing, and textile craftsmanship. India was historically the epicenter of the global textile trade — celebrated across Rome, Greece, and Europe for muslin, calico, chintz, and cashmere weaves that later formed the bedrock of Western high fashion.
* **-IÉ / -IER:** The prestigious French suffix. In Parisian fashion and craft terminology, the `-ier` / `-ié` construct signifies the **Master Artisan** or craftsman dedicated to a singular discipline:
  * *Couturier* — Master of tailoring & dressmaking
  * *Joaillier* — Master of high jewelry
  * *Bottier* — Master shoemaker
  * *Atelier* — The master craftsman's workshop
* **Synthesized Meaning:** **"The Master Artisan of Clothing"** — Honoring the ancestral origin of fine textiles while projecting an uncompromising, modern luxury aesthetic.

---

### 2. Phonetic Guide & Pronunciation
* **Pronunciation:** `/vɑːs.tʁi.e/` (**"vas-tree-ay"**)
* **How It Is Read Naturally:**
  * The acute accent `É` signals the French long vowel sound, naturally steering international and fashion-conscious audiences to pronounce it as **"vas-tree-ay"** (analogous to *Hermès*, *Céline*, *Chloé*, or *Moët*).
  * In Latin-rooted phonetics, the cadence evokes classic luxury houses like **Cartier** (*car-tee-ay*), **Atelier** (*ah-tel-ee-ay*), and **Gaultier** (*goal-tee-ay*), eliminating any need for phonetic subtitles or awkward explanations.

## ✨ Brand Philosophy & Vision

VASTRIÉ is built on the belief that dressing well is not about fleeting micro-trends; it is an exact geometry of proportions, fit, color harmony, and context.

* **Craftsmanship Meets Computation:** We studied Savile Row tailoring blueprints, Milanese *sprezzatura*, and classic menswear rules to build a proprietary **Fashion Knowledge Graph**.
* **Zero Decision Fatigue:** Modern professionals, leaders, and creatives are freed from closet anxiety through intelligent, context-aware outfit curations tailored to their exact body profile, location, climate, and calendar events.
* **Wardrobe as an Asset:** Rather than encouraging endless fast-fashion consumption, VASTRIÉ catalogs existing wardrobe items, highlights true essentials, and recommends timeless acquisitions that complete versatile capsules.

---

## 🚀 Key Modules & Architecture

1. **The Digital Atelier (`/wardrobe`):** High-fidelity digital cataloging of individual garments, categorizing items by fabric, season, formality, and color palette.
2. **Contextual AI Stylist (`/outfits`):** Real-time outfit composition driven by weather/climate parameters (e.g., Summer 75°F+, Spring Trans, Crisp Autumn), event formality, and color theory.
3. **Style Profile & Biometrics (`/profile`):** Comprehensive taste, fit preference, body silhouette, and lifestyle mapping.
4. **Curated Discovery (`/discover`):** Editorial-grade lookbooks and capsule wardrobe inspirations.
5. **Intelligent Localization & Regional Detection:** Auto-detects user region to default to French (`FR`) for access from France and English (`EN`) for all other international regions, accompanied by an interactive luxury `[ EN | FR ]` switcher.
